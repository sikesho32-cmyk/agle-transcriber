    📖AGLE Rejection-Free Guidelines

    1. Timestamps & Title

    • Audio length must be included directly in the title header line.
    • No microseconds in timestamps.

    2. 7-Word Topic

    • Topic headers must contain exactly 7 words.

    3. Verbatim Modes

    • **Edited**: Remove fillers (um, uh) and false starts.
    • **Strict**: Keep all utterances intact.

    4. UI Elements

    • Always capitalize and double-quote buttons (e.g., click "Submit").
